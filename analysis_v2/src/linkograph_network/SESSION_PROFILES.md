# Session profiles — the eight added sessions

Each profile leads with what the session looked like, then the evidence. NES_S3 and
NES_S10 are already written up elsewhere.

"Teamed anywhere" counts people who joined a team in any session, not just this one.
Quotes are Gemini's cited `evidence`; full context is in
`transcript_evidence/evidence_<LABEL>.md`.

---

## NES_S4 — 2020NES, 5 Nov 2020 · 3 in-room teams, 2 funded

**What happened.** The most successful room in the set. Thirteen people on beyond-CO2
chemistry; three teams formed inside the room and two were funded. Five people left with
an in-room teammate — Carlos Morales-Guio with Katie Knowles (NES19), Chong Liu with both
Nanette Boyle (NES29) and Sen Zhang (NES9). Ten of the thirteen joined a team somewhere.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **One person carried the room and it still worked.** Charles McCrory's weighted degree
  is 24, double the next person (Adam Holewinski, 12), and centralization is 0.705 — high.
  But participation was near-even (0.948) and the self-link ratio is the lowest in the
  whole set at 0.073. So Charles was a hub people built *through*, not someone talking to
  himself.
- **Building dominates over challenging.** 28 of 55 links are `extends_existing_idea`,
  against only 4 critiques. Additive, not adversarial.
- **The links are unusually well grounded** — 17 named, 30 on-table, only 8 fallback.

**Evidence.** Michael Nippe on *"the electrocatalytic generation of food for enzymes…
efficient systems for NAD+ to NADH"*, which Gemini records as building directly on Chong
Liu's *"nature has enzymes to do methane to liquid… maybe they just need an energy source
like electrochemistry."* Katie Knowles then picks up Michael explicitly — *"build off an
idea that Michael just said… using electrochemistry more broadly."* Three people, three
turns, one thread widening.

## MZT_S5 — 2021MZT, 30 Sep 2021 · 2 in-room teams, both funded

**What happened.** Twelve people on pathogen surveillance. Two teams formed and both were
funded — the best funding rate in the set. But **no in-room teammates are flagged**, which
means the people who teamed up weren't both idea-speakers in this room.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **The most centralized room here, and it worked anyway.** Pilar Fernandez has a weighted
  degree of 26 against 12 for the next person, centralization 0.712. Every one of the top
  five speaker pairs includes Pilar — she is in the middle of essentially all the
  building.
- **Highest new-idea count in the set** (16 new ideas from 61 idea acts), so the room was
  generating, not just refining.
- **Self-linking is moderate** (0.204) — Pilar was building on others as much as herself.

**Evidence.** Matt Erdman ↔ Pilar is the heaviest pair in any of the eight, 8 links, and
it runs both ways (5 one direction, 3 the other) — a genuine exchange rather than one
person orbiting another. Matt: *"What's the actionable outcome of surveillance?"* against
Pilar's *"how we achieve sustainability of the surveillance system."*

## MND_S5 — 2022MND, 7 Apr 2022 · 2 in-room teams, 1 funded

**What happened.** The smallest room (8 idea-speakers of 11 people), on the gut
microbiome. Two teams, one funded, and this is the only session besides NES_S4 with
confirmed in-room teammates — Faranak Fattahi and Iliyan Iliev left together on MND5, the
funded team.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **The flattest network in the set.** Centralization 0.462, max betweenness 0.187, both
  the lowest of the eight. No hub — Iliyan (13), Erin Longbrake (12) and Rosa
  Krajmalnik-Brown (12) are effectively tied.
- **Deepest single thread in the set** — an 8-step build chain, the longest anywhere
  including the originals.
- **But the highest self-link ratio** (0.262). More building on one's own earlier points
  than any other session here.
- Zero bridging moves — nobody joined two separate idea threads.

**Evidence.** Faranak Fattahi asking *"are there ways that microbes could trigger higher
production of mucus, or is that worse for their own survival?"* — building on Rosa's
introduction of mucus. Faranak went on to form the funded team with Iliyan, and both sit
at the centre of this network.

## CMC_S11 — 2021CMC, 8 Oct 2021 · 1 in-room team, 0 funded

**What happened.** Thirteen people on cellular signalling and scale. One team formed and
it wasn't funded — the useful case for separating "formed a team" from "got funded." Only
6 of 13 joined a team anywhere, the joint-lowest rate here.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **A genuinely conceptual session.** The most abstract discussion of the eight — what
  counts as "self," deterministic versus stochastic development, how big a system must be
  before a property emerges.
- **Three-way centre.** Rigoberto Hernandez (16), Gulcin Pekkurnaz (15) and Wenjing Wang
  (13) are close, with the highest betweenness of the team-forming sessions (0.343).
- **Most argumentative room** — 7 critiques against 17 extends, the highest ratio of
  challenge to build in the set.
- **Highest cross-chunk linking anywhere** (56%), meaning ideas persisted and were
  returned to across long stretches.

**Evidence.** Wenjing Wang: *"I want to argue against that the size of the neuron is so
different"* — directly answering Gulcin's *"if size matter that much, why neurons are the
exception?"* A disagreement that stayed on one thread rather than splitting it.

## NES_S8 — 2021NES, 5 Nov 2021 · 0 in-room teams

**What happened. This is the clearest NES_S10 repeat in the set.** Zero teams formed
inside the room, yet **8 of 10 people teamed up elsewhere** — 7 of the 9 network nodes
carry a team outcome. A productive room whose collaborations all landed outside it.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **The smallest idea output of the eight** — 40 idea acts, 10 new ideas, 34 links.
- **Two co-leads rather than one hub.** Emily Carter (14) and Wilson Smith (13) are level,
  and betweenness is the *lowest* in the set (0.131) — nobody was a bottleneck.
- **The least grounded session here.** 11 of 34 links are fallback, only 9 named. Read its
  network numbers with that caveat.
- Concern-raising is elevated (5 of 34 moves) relative to its size.

**Evidence.** Wilson Smith: *"back to **Linsey's** point… having local identification
under reaction conditions."* He names his target out loud, which is exactly the signal the
linker's strongest rule looks for — and it resolved correctly to Linsey Seitz.

## ABI_S1 — 2021ABI, 21 May 2021 · 0 in-room teams

**What happened.** Eleven people on biological imaging. No in-room teams, and only 5 of 11
teamed up anywhere — though 3 of those were funded.

**Read this session with more caution than the others.** It is the only 2021ABI session
that clears the material floor, and ABI as a conference runs thin (median 26 idea
utterances against 46 overall). It also has the lowest naming rate of any conference
(11%), so its links lean hardest on the weaker rules.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **The most lopsided room in the set.** Centralization 0.711 and max betweenness 0.454,
  the highest of the eight. Brian Pogue (15) and Katharine White (12) dominate; the next
  is 6.
- **Lowest participation evenness** (0.819) and **shortest build chains** (5), with zero
  bridging moves. Ideas got raised and dropped rather than developed.
- **Only 5 of 34 links are named** — the weakest evidence base of the eight.
- One person appeared under two spellings (`Brian Spring` / `Bryan Spring`); merged
  explicitly, which moved max betweenness from 0.548 to 0.454.

**Evidence.** Brian Pogue's *"like a multi-scale approach"* building on Shannon Quine's
*"how do we have a molecular dynamics situation for imaging data."* Note how short the
contributions are — characteristic of this session.

## MND_S15 — 2021MND, 23 Apr 2021 · 0 in-room teams

**What happened.** Twelve people on gut motility, stress and the microbiome. No in-room
teams; 5 of 12 teamed up elsewhere.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **The most evenly distributed session in the entire ten** — participation entropy 0.957,
  higher than NES_S3's 0.953. Nobody dominated: Abhishek Shrivastava and Ying-Hui Chou tie
  at 11.
- **And the best-grounded links anywhere** — 29 on-table, 8 named, and only **1 fallback
  link out of 38**.
- **Yet no teams formed.** This is the sharpest counter-example in the set: the most even
  participation and the cleanest evidence, and nobody left with a collaborator from the
  room.
- Fewest new ideas of the eight (11), and short chains (5).

**Evidence.** A list being built collaboratively — Ying-Hui Chou offers *"like traumatic
brain injury, that could be a stress"*, Ali Keshavarzian extends to *"vaginal environment
is under tremendous amount of stress as well"*, Stavroula Hatzios adds *"and antibiotics
too."* Three people, one list, three turns. Textbook additive building that produced no
team.

## SLU_S5 — 2021SLU, 11 Jun 2021 · 0 in-room teams

**What happened.** The largest room (13 people, 11 idea-speakers), on astrobiology and
biosignatures. No in-room teams and only 5 of 13 teamed up elsewhere — with just 1 funded,
the lowest of the eight.

**What the idea work looked like.**

- **Most synthesis-heavy session in the set** — 13 of 47 moves are
  `synthesizes_contributions`, plus 5 `connects_methods`. People were actively pulling
  threads together.
- **Middling centralization** (0.588) with Maggie Osburn (16) ahead of Morgan Raven (12)
  and Andro Rios (11).
- **Well grounded** — 13 named, 29 on-table, 5 fallback.
- **High cross-chunk linking** (53%) — ideas persisted and were returned to across the
  session.
- One person appeared as `Tori Hoehler` and `Tori Hoeler`; merged, which moved
  centralization from 0.625 to 0.588.

**Evidence.** Andro Rios on *"a continuity of precursor molecules so that you see that
same isotopic signature in the same precursor compound"*, building on Maggie Osburn's
*"one of the biggest battles we fight is the abiotic system mimicking the biological
system in terms of the isotopic fractionations."* A methodological problem and a proposed
handle on it.

## Across the eight

**The tidy story does not hold.** MND_S15 has the most even participation of all ten
sessions and the cleanest links, and produced nothing. MZT_S5 is the second most
centralized and produced two funded teams. Centralization does not separate the outcomes
in this set — if anything it runs the wrong way.

**And nothing else separates them either.** The two sessions with confirmed in-room
teammates, NES_S4 and MND_S5, sit at opposite ends of self-linking — 0.073 (lowest of all
ten) and 0.262 (highest of the team sessions). No metric in the block cleanly divides
sessions that produced in-room teams from those that didn't. That is the honest headline
from these eight, and it is worth stating rather than hunting for a pattern that isn't
there at n=10.

**Two sessions to caveat when presenting:** ABI_S1 (thin conference, weakest link
evidence) and NES_S8 (11 of 34 links are fallback).
