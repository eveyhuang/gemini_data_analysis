## NES_S4 (2020NES · 5 Nov 2020) — 3 teams formed, 2 funded

**What happened**
- Thirteen people on beyond-CO2 chemistry. The most successful room in the set.
- Five left with an in-room teammate: Carlos Morales-Guio + Katie Knowles (NES19); Chong
  Liu with both Nanette Boyle (NES29) and Sen Zhang (NES9)
- 10 of 13 joined a team somewhere

**How they built on ideas**
- **One person carried the room and it still worked.** Charles McCrory's weighted degree
  is 24, double the next (Adam Holewinski, 12). Centralization 0.705 — high. But
  participation was near-even (0.948) and self-linking the lowest of all ten (0.073). A
  hub people built *through*, not someone talking to himself.
- **Additive, not adversarial** — 28 of 55 links are extends, only 4 critiques
- Best-grounded links of the eight: 17 named / 30 on-table / 8 fallback

**Evidence — a thread widening across three people**
> Chong Liu: *"nature has enzymes to do methane to liquid… maybe they just need an energy
> source like electrochemistry"*
> → Michael Nippe extends it to cofactor generation
> → Katie Knowles: *"build off an idea that Michael just said… using electrochemistry more
> broadly"*

## MND_S15 (2021MND · 23 Apr 2021) — 0 in-room teams · **the counter-example**

**What happened**
- Twelve people on gut motility, stress and the microbiome
- No in-room teams; 5 of 12 teamed up elsewhere

**How they built on ideas**
- **The most evenly distributed session of all ten** — participation entropy 0.957, higher
  than NES_S3's 0.953. Abhishek Shrivastava and Ying-Hui Chou tie at 11.
- **The best-grounded links anywhere** — 29 on-table, 8 named, **1 fallback out of 38**
- **And no team formed.** The sharpest counter-example in the set: the most even
  participation and the cleanest evidence, and nobody left with a collaborator from the room.
- Fewest new ideas of the eight (11); short chains (5)

**Evidence — a list built collaboratively, three people, three turns**
> Ying-Hui Chou: *"Like traumatic brain injury, that could be a stress."*
> → Abhishek Shrivastava: *"changes in oxygen are definitely one type of stress"*
> → Lisa Ryno: *"how would you interface that with one of these organoid models?"*
> → Carolina Tropini: *"it's key to find the right model for the question that is asked"*

**Say this one out loud:** textbook additive building that produced nothing.

## Optional closing slide — what the eight show together

**No metric separates the sessions that produced in-room teams from those that didn't.**

- MND_S15 has the most even participation of all ten and produced nothing
- MZT_S5 is the second most centralized and produced two funded teams
- The two sessions with confirmed in-room teammates sit at **opposite** ends of
  self-linking (0.073 and 0.262)

The two things that do track the outcome are cross-session, not per-session: **idea
density** (r = 0.63) and **how many people use more than one kind of idea-move** (r = 0.68).

**Caveat when presenting:** ABI_S1 (thin conference, weakest link evidence) and NES_S8
(11 of 34 links are fallback).
